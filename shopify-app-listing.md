### Testing Instructions

```
To test this app:

1. Install the app from the Shopify Admin
2. On the Dashboard, follow the Setup Guide checklist to complete onboarding
3. Go to Indexing, paste any product URL, and click Submit to see instant IndexNow submission to Bing and Yandex
4. Click "Reindex All Products" to batch-submit all store products
5. Go to Settings → Connections → click "Connect with Google" to link Google Search Console and GA4
6. Go to Settings → Connections → click "Connect with Bing" to link Bing Webmaster Tools
7. Return to Dashboard to see search traffic stats, AI traffic breakdown, and top Bing keywords
8. Go to AI Visibility → Keywords tab → add a keyword → click "Run Scan Now" to check brand mentions
9. Go to AI Visibility → Schema tab → paste a product URL → click "Audit" to see structured data scores
10. Go to AI Visibility → AI Traffic tab to see sessions from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini

Note: Google and Bing OAuth require the store to have live traffic for full data. Schema audits work on any public URL immediately.
```
